Why can we offer smarter prices?

Instead of following a fixed retail pricing model, we evaluate each lot, model and product condition separately and pass a part of the inventory-level price advantage to our customers.

Clear Product Classification

We classify our inventory based on product condition so customers know what they are enquiring about.

D1 — FRESH CONDITION

Fresh & Clean Inventory

Products in fresh condition with no visible scratches, marks or cosmetic damage. Packaging and product condition may vary depending on the individual inventory lot.

Product status and availability are verified before finalizing the order.

D2 — OPEN / COSMETIC

Open Box & Cosmetic-Grade Inventory

Products that may have opened packaging, minor scratches, cosmetic marks, handling marks, customer-return history or similar non-functional cosmetic differences.

Condition is disclosed and discussed with the customer before purchase.

Our D2 category does not automatically mean a used product. Each item is evaluated individually, and its actual condition is communicated before the sale.

Bulk Inventory. Better Value.

Our products are sourced through bulk-purchased e-commerce and retail inventory lots. These lots can include fresh inventory, opened-box inventory, customer-return inventory and products with cosmetic packaging or handling differences, depending on the specific lot.

This inventory model allows us to operate differently from traditional retail stores and offer competitive prices on selected products.
